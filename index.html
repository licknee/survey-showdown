SURVEY SHOWDOWN

A Family Feud-style party game

1
Set up: Name your two teams, then add survey-style questions with answers and point values. Upload a CSV or type them in manually.
2
Face off: Each round, the host reads the question aloud. Teams try to guess the most popular answers on the board.
3
Reveal & strike: The host reveals correct answers (click the tile or press 1-8). Wrong guesses get a strike — three strikes and the other team can steal!
4
Score: Award the round's points to the winning team. The team with the most points at the end wins!
Tip: Open the Audience Display on a TV or projector so everyone can see the board while the host controls the game.
